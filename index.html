03 — Experience

Where I've worked.

2026 — Present

Research Project Team Member

Neuromatch Impact Scholars Program · Johns Hopkins University

Collaborating with Yueh-Chen Chiang (Johns Hopkins) and Yacine Belkhodja on Latent Representation for Neural Prediction — testing self-supervised learning objectives for neural data and benchmarking learned representations with the Neural Latents Benchmark and FALCON.

2026 — Present

Working Group Contributor

ManyBabies Consortium · ManyBabies 3 & 8

Active member of the ManyBabies 8 collaboration and its working groups; regular participant in the ManyBabies 3 Data Analyses/Curation Working Group.

2026

Deep Learning Project Team Member

Neuromatch Academy · BOLD Brains

Completed the intensive three-week Deep Learning program with a six-person research team. Presented “Atlas of Neural Representation Learning” and built reproducible AlexNet experiments testing BOLD-guided representation learning on the Kay natural-image fMRI dataset.

2025 — 2026

Neurobehavioral Analyst

Child Mind Institute

Manage, extract, process and analyse fMRI datasets, and build automation scripts in Bash, R and Python. Run visual quality control on imaging data, support investigators’ research writing, and prepare and distribute biweekly internal data assessments.

2024 — 2025

SURFiN Fellow · Research Assistant

Simons Foundation · Princeton Neuroscience Institute

Investigated hidden circuit dynamics within recurrent neural networks. Developed linear decoders, analysis workflows and computational models for neural and biological process analysis, and presented findings at the fellowship symposium.

2024 — 2025

Junior Research Fellow

Wilson Center · Washington, DC

Conducted independent research on Bangladesh’s government through news coverage, interviews and expert input. Authored the forthcoming chapter “Legal and Institutional Barriers to Justice and Accountability” in Change and Justice in Bangladesh (De Gruyter Brill, 2027).

2023 — 2025

Research Assistant

Autism & Neurodevelopment Lab · Montclair State

Collected EEG data for an ERP face-perception study using REDCap, Curry 7, MATLAB and MNE-Python. Administered SRS, SSIS and ADOS assessments, and contributed to a forthcoming journal article and an APA 2025 poster.

2023 — 2025

Research Assistant

Project AROS Lab · Montclair State

Created a digital study-abroad curriculum using a challenge-based learning framework, and used Python and ArcGIS for spatial analysis projects.
